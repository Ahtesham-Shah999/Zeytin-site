A grill station, not a kitchen.

Zeytin opened with one rule: nothing leaves the grill unless it's been over real charcoal. No shortcuts, no gas flame standing in for fire. Our cooks learned the trade in Levantine and Anatolian kitchens, and it shows in every skewer.

Sixty-four seats inside, eight more on the terrace when the weather agrees. Come for a quiet weeknight dinner or bring the whole table for a Friday night feast.
